Questions

Good to know.

Do you support third-party attribution?

Yes. BitNode works with mainstream mobile measurement workflows through postbacks, event mapping, and campaign identifiers.

Which buying models are available?

Campaigns can be structured around CPI, CPA, CPM, CPC, or custom commercial terms depending on market, supply, and KPI.

Can publishers connect inventory?

Yes. We review in-app supply, ad formats, market coverage, traffic quality, and integration options.

Where is BitNode based?

BitNode is a Hong Kong company serving cross-border mobile advertising demand and supply partners worldwide.
